Probabilistic Interpretation & Limitations

Deepfake Video Forensics produces statistical probabilities based on learned patterns from the FaceForensics++ benchmark. It is optimized for facial reenactment, deepfake synthesis, and facial attribute tampering. It does not establish absolute proof and should be complemented by multi-modal forensics (audio analysis, metadata/C2PA verification, and chain of custody inspection).
